Fistbump

Fistbump lets you own and resolve names on the blockchain. To browse Fistbump names directly in your browser, we need to set up two things:

Root Certificate
A local certificate authority so your browser trusts Fistbump names over HTTPS. This is stored on your machine and never leaves it.
Name Resolver
A proxy configuration that tells your browser how to find Fistbump names. Names that aren't registered with traditional DNS authorities are resolved through your local node.
